CHINO Pickleball Courts

Court policies

For a safe, enjoyable, and respectful playing environment.

Booking & payment

  1. Full Payment

    Full payment is required to secure your reservation. Bookings are confirmed only after payment has been received and verified.

  2. No Refunds, Cancellations, or Rebooking

    Once a booking is confirmed, refunds, cancellations, and rebooking are not allowed, except when the court is deemed unplayable due to unfavorable weather conditions. Weather-related refunds or rebooking are subject to management confirmation.

  3. Outdoor Facility

    Chino Pickleball Courts is an outdoor facility. Players are encouraged to check weather conditions before booking and come prepared for outdoor play.

  4. Late Arrivals

    Late arrivals will still follow the original booked time. No extension will be given for late arrival.

  5. End of Booking

    Please vacate the court promptly once your booked time ends to allow the next players to enter on schedule.

Player conduct

  1. Respectful Conduct

    Please treat other players, staff, guests, and the facility with respect and courtesy.

  2. Proper Behavior

    Foul language, harassment, fighting, unsportsmanlike behavior, and other disruptive conduct will not be tolerated.

  3. Speaker Use — Starting 10:00 PM

    Please minimize the use and volume of speakers starting at 10:00 PM to maintain a comfortable and peaceful environment around the facility.

  4. Smoking, Vaping & Illegal Substances

    Smoking, vaping, and the use or possession of illegal substances are strictly prohibited within the court area.

  5. Pickleball Etiquette

    Observe proper pickleball etiquette, practice good sportsmanship, and play fairly at all times.

  6. Use Only Your Booked Court

    Players may use only the court assigned or booked to them. Playing on another court, even if it is vacant, is not allowed and may result in additional charges.

Facility use

  1. Proper Attire & Footwear

    Appropriate sports attire and proper non-marking court shoes must be worn while playing. Footwear that may damage the court surface is not allowed.

  2. Food & Drinks

    Food and drinks should be kept outside the playing area. Water in secure, spill-proof containers is allowed courtside.

  3. Keep the Area Clean

    Please keep the court and surrounding areas clean. Dispose of all trash properly in the designated bins before leaving.

Safety & liability

  1. Play at Your Own Risk

    Players use the courts and facilities at their own risk. Chino Pickleball Courts will not be responsible for injuries, accidents, or damages arising from the use of the facility.

  2. Personal Belongings

    Players are responsible for their own personal belongings. Management will not be responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged items.

Management reserves the right to remind, warn, or ask any player or guest to leave the premises for violations of these policies or for behavior that compromises the safety, comfort, or enjoyment of others.
