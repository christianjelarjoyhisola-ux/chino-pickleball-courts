CHINO Court Policies

For a safe, enjoyable, and respectful play environment.

Booking & payment

  1. Full payment is required to secure your reservation. Bookings are confirmed only after payment has been received and verified.
  2. No refunds, cancellations, or rebooking once the booking is confirmed, except when the court is deemed unplayable due to weather conditions. Weather-related refunds or rebooking are subject to management confirmation.
  3. Chino Pickleball Courts is an outdoor facility. Please check weather conditions before booking and come prepared for outdoor play.
  4. Late arrival will still follow the original booked time. No extension will be given for late arrival.
  5. Please vacate the court promptly after your scheduled time to allow the next players to enter on time.

Player conduct

  1. Be respectful to all players, staff, and the community. Let’s keep the court a welcoming place for everyone.
  2. No foul language, harassment, or unsportsmanlike behavior.
  3. Please minimize the use of speakers starting 10:00 PM to maintain a comfortable environment for all players.
  4. The use of illegal substances, smoking, and vaping is not allowed within the court area.
  5. Follow proper pickleball etiquette and play fairly.
  6. Use only the court you booked. Playing on any other court, even if vacant, is not allowed and will be charged accordingly.

Facility use

  1. Proper sports attire and non-marking court shoes are required to help maintain the playing surface.
  2. Food and drinks must be kept outside the playing area. Water in secure containers is allowed.
  3. Please keep the court and surrounding area clean. Dispose of all trash properly in the designated bins before leaving.

Safety & liability

  1. Players use the facility at their own risk. Chino Pickleball Courts will not be liable for injuries, accidents, or any damages resulting from the use of the facility.
  2. Players are responsible for their personal belongings. Management will not be liable for lost, stolen, or damaged items.
