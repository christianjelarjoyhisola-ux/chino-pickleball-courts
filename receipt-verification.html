Receipt verification

Check that your payment details and receipt are correct before continuing.

For GCash, tap Download on the completed receipt and upload the original image. Include the recipient, amount, reference, and payment date and time.

Selecting “Continue — Verify Payment” requests Google Cloud Vision analysis of your uploaded receipt for payment verification and fraud prevention. Choosing a file uploads it securely; analysis begins when you continue.

When automatic verification is available, a complete, matching receipt can confirm your payment automatically. Unclear or mismatched details stay pending for the court owner to review.

If a detail is unclear, CHINO can make additional readings automatically. A temporary checking-service failure may also be retried. Keep the payment window open while checking; do not make another payment.

If checking still cannot finish, the owner can review the saved receipt. A service failure is different from a payment mismatch. Your result will explain which needs attention.

CHINO keeps the receipt and audit record private and retains them only as long as needed for disputes, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal requirements. Contact CHINO staff for privacy requests.

The separate Court Policies explain booking, payment, weather exceptions, and court rules.
